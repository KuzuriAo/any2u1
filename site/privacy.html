Privacy policy

Short version: your files are converted on your device and never leave it. I collect nothing.

This covers the any2u1 browser extension, version 0.1.0 and later. Last updated November 3, 2026.

Your files

The extension reads a 3MF file, or a zip of them, only when you ask it to convert one. It converts the file inside your browser and saves the result to your downloads folder. The file, and anything in it, is never sent anywhere.

Requests it makes

Besides loading its own files, the extension makes one kind of request, and only when you click to convert a download: it fetches that one file again, from the address your browser downloaded it from. It sends no cookies and no referrer with it. It doesn't fetch anything else, and it doesn't contact me or anyone else. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or accounts.

What it keeps on your device

  • Your settings, in the browser's extension storage: whether offers are on, how they're shown, the sites you turned them off for, your conversion options, the list of Snapmaker Orca versions it last saw, and a note about a new version until you dismiss it.
  • Downloads waiting for an answer: each one's file name, the name of the site it came from, the number the browser gave the download, and when it finished, plus which window shows them, in storage the browser clears when it closes. The download's address isn't kept, since a signed link can work like a password; the extension looks it up from the browser when you click.

None of this leaves your device. Removing the extension removes it.

Site access you allow

If a site's server won't let the extension read a file, the extension can ask your permission for that one host. It uses that access only to fetch a file you chose to convert. The extension's settings page lists every host you allowed, and removes one. Some browsers remember an approval until the extension is removed.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't read or change the pages you visit, it doesn't track what you browse, and it doesn't sell or share data, because it has none to share.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions go to the project's issue tracker.
