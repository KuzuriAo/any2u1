How to offer

Pin the any2u1 button to your toolbar, or you won't see the number: the browser hides it on an unpinned extension. Clicking the button opens the small window.

A download manager that takes over your downloads hides them from the browser, so there's no offer for them. The same goes if a saved file has a random name or no .3mf ending. For any saved file, click the toolbar button and choose it.
